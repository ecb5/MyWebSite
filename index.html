About the authors

Robert Rogers is a SUNY Distinguished Teaching Professor of Mathematics at SUNY Fredonia, where he has been a faculty member since 1987. He holds a BS in mathematics, with secondary education certification, from SUNY Buffalo State, an MS in mathematics from Syracuse University, and a PhD in mathematics from the University at Buffalo. Active in the mathematics teaching community, he is a past president of the Association of Mathematics Teachers of New York State, a former chair and governor of the Seaway Section of the Mathematical Association of America (MAA), a former editor of the New York State Mathematics Teachers’ Journal, and a Distinguished Lecturer for the MAA Seaway Section. His honors include the SUNY Fredonia President’s Award for Excellence in Teaching, the Seaway Section’s Clarence Stephens Award for Teaching Excellence and its Distinguished Service Award, and membership in the New York State Mathematics Educators’ Hall of Fame.

Eugene Boman is Professor Emeritus at the Harrisburg campus of The Pennsylvania State University. He has taught college mathematics since 1984, when he began graduate study at the University of Connecticut. He earned his MA (1986) and PhD (1993) there, after receiving his BA from Reed College in 1984. In 2008, the editors of Mathematics Magazine presented him with the Carl B. Allendorfer Award for excellence in expository mathematical writing, for his article “Mom! There’s an Astroid in My Closet” (vol. 80, 2007, pp. 104–111).

Our textbooks are part of Milne Open Textbooks, a catalog of open textbooks written and peer-reviewed by SUNY faculty and staff. Milne Library Publishing at SUNY Geneseo manages and maintains the catalog, giving instructors a dependable place to find free, vetted course materials.
